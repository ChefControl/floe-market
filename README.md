# Floe Market

A cozy 3D idle tycoon in three stages, coming soon to Steam. Start with a fish stall on the ice, grow it into Floe Sushi, a conveyor-belt restaurant with rice terraces on the hillside, then answer the knock under the dock: Mr. Fugu's illegal sumo ring is waiting in the cellar. Cook chanko for the crowd, turn each boss's fans before the bell, and buy him out.

**Coming soon to Steam.** The free browser version closed in November 2026.
