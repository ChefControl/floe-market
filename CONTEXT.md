# Floe Market

A cozy idle game: the player runs a fish market, grows it into the Floe Sushi restaurant, and can gamble their cash on a casino riverboat in the harbor.

## Language

### Casino

**Round**:
One play at a casino game, from the stake going down to the payout: a blackjack round, a roulette round, a slots round.
_Avoid_: Hand, spin, pull, deal (these name what is seen at the table, not the play as a whole)

**Stake**:
The money the player puts on a round, built from chips or all in.
_Avoid_: Bet (roulette's bet is the colour picked, not the money), wager

**Shoe**:
Blackjack's six shuffled decks, which last across many rounds until the cut card comes out.
_Avoid_: Deck (a shoe holds six)

**Cut card**:
The card the player places in a freshly shuffled shoe; when it is reached, the shoe is reshuffled before the next round.

**Burn**:
The one card discarded unseen from the top of each new shoe.

### Cooking Hazard (the arcade cabinet's game, docs/cooking-hazard.md)

**Run**:
One trip out in the van, from the drop-off to extraction or death.
_Avoid_: Level, mission, expedition (the sketch's word; the game says run)

**Taste**:
One of the six (Spicy, Bitter, Sour, Salty, Sweet, Umami); each is an element (Fire, Poison, Acid, Electric, Freeze, Lure).
_Avoid_: Flavour, type

**Coat**:
The taste a zombie wears after a hit, one at a time, fading in about 4 seconds.
_Avoid_: Debuff, mark, aura

**Ring**:
How full a building taste (Salty, Sweet, Umami) is on a zombie, drawn at its feet; full, it goes off.
_Avoid_: Meter, buildup bar

**Technique**:
What a second, different taste does to a coated zombie: one of 15, named after a real one (Brûléed, Pickled).
_Avoid_: Combo, reaction, dish (the zombie is the dish)

**Dish**:
A premade dish taken into a run in a dish slot, thrown after typing its recipe code; it has servings.
_Avoid_: Stratagem, ability, grenade

**Serving**:
One use of a dish; the stars at the counter set how many.

**Recipe code**:
The arrows typed to serve a dish, shown as a recipe ticket of cooking moves.
_Avoid_: Combo, input

**Order ticket**:
A special zombie's two or three tastes, in order; hitting them all cooks it on the spot.
_Avoid_: Weakness, allergy (the allergy is the theme, the ticket is the rule)

**Garnish**:
What a technique drops: picked up, it boosts the gun's next shots.

**Horde**:
The ordinary zombies: HP only, growing with the clock.
