# Floe Market

A cozy idle game: the player runs a fish market, grows it into the Floe Sushi restaurant, and can gamble their cash on a casino riverboat in the harbor.

## Language

### Casino

**Round**:
One play at a casino game, from the stake going down to the payout: a blackjack round, a roulette round, a slots round.
_Avoid_: Hand, spin, pull, deal (these name what is seen at the table, not the play as a whole)

**Stake**:
The money the player puts on a round, built from chips or all in.
_Avoid_: Bet (roulette's bet is the colour picked, not the money), wager

**Shoe**:
Blackjack's six shuffled decks, which last across many rounds until the cut card comes out.
_Avoid_: Deck (a shoe holds six)

**Cut card**:
The card the player places in a freshly shuffled shoe; when it is reached, the shoe is reshuffled before the next round.

**Burn**:
The one card discarded unseen from the top of each new shoe.
