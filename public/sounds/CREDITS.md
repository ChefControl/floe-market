# Sounds

The ring's crowd (stage 3) is recorded: the rest of the game's sound is synthesised in the browser (`src/audio.ts`).
The recordings are cut down and mixed to mono for the game. The game credits them in its settings, under Credits & Copyrights.

Changes from the originals: trimmed, faded, mixed down to mono, levelled, layered (the murmur and the roar), and the murmur, chant and roar crossfaded into loops. The murmur is also muffled a little (low-passed at 3.5 kHz), and the swell is a stretch of cheering given a rising level and a muffled-to-open sound.

Two earlier cuts had music in the background of their recordings and were replaced (October 2026): the murmur (from "Crowd Cheering Sounds - 10 - Ambience") and the swell (from "05 - Soft cheering - I"). Check any new crowd recording for music before using it.
